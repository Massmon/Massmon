In my ESE 1110 class on Thursday we had a guest speaker named Amehja. I was busy as usual trying to finish my procrastinated OCaml homework, but she said something that then made me freeze: "Someone once told me you can do anything but not everything."

Afterwards, I closed my laptop and began to pay attention. Amehja went on to talk about time management, her goals to work at NASA, and how important it was to have a study group throughout her time at Penn. However, leaving class that day, that one maxim she said stuck with me. Coming to college broadened my whole world. Everything seems so cool, and even within my major of Electrical Engineering there are many, many subfields. There is so much to explore that I feel like I am getting tugged every which way, but I also feel like I have no time to explore these fields to the depths that they deserve. This feeling, while I am ashamed to admit it, leads to paralysis in my daily life. I seek out my 3DS, outings with friends, or anime to get the thought that I "have to make a choice with my time" out of my head.

Amehja's talk really helped me see my current self in a new light, and will hopefully shape my new approach to college as I prioritize learning what seems the most fun (and think later!). Thanks for reading my yap session :)
